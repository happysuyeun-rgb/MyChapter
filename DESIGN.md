## Product visual identity

**Paper × Botanical × Vintage × Editorial × AI**

MY CHAPTER should feel like a quiet **digital publishing desk**, not a generic wellness/journal app.

## Core colors

- Ink Brown: `#3B352D`
- Warm Ivory: `#F7F2E8`
- Sage: `#7C8D78`
- Terracotta: `#C87959`

## UI rules

- Prefer editorial lists, hairline dividers, book/page surfaces and asymmetric hierarchy.
- Avoid repeated rounded cards and card-in-card composition.
- Use serif typography for book/editorial hierarchy, sans-serif for operational UI.
- Keep one dominant CTA per screen.
- PAGE is a separate brand character; functional icons must not imitate PAGE.
- Avoid decorative emoji as navigation or feature icons.
- Book covers may use subtle book-like corner radius and shadow as a semantic exception.
- Mobile first: verify 320 / 375 / 414 px and safe-area bottom actions.
- Motion must be restrained and informative.
